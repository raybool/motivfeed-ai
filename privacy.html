Privacy

Last updated: September 23, 2026.

Short version: this website is a product-research prototype. Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose “Allow analytics.” We do not send your free-form search text or open-ended survey response to Google Analytics.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage, marketing attribution and completion of the product-research funnel. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization are disabled.

What we measure

We may measure page views, clicks, broad motivation categories, survey selections, demo interactions, completion events, device/campaign information supplied by Google Analytics, and technical error signals.

What we intentionally do not send to GA4

  • The raw text you type into the motivation search box.
  • The optional open-ended survey response.
  • Email addresses, names or other intentionally collected personal identifiers.

Local prototype data

The latest completed survey response is currently stored in your browser using localStorage so it can be copied during moderated research. It is not transmitted to our own backend by this prototype.

Change your analytics choice

Return to the homepage and choose “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can switch analytics consent on or off there.

Third-party media

The phone mockup may load demo stock-video assets from third-party content delivery networks. These are visual product demonstrations and are separate from Google Analytics.
